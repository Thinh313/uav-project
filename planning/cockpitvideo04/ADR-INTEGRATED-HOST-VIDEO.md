# ADR: reuse the cockpit and flight contracts, isolate the media topology

Accepted within the user's scope, 22 October 2026. HOSTVIDEO03 proved isolated Android decoding in two cold cohorts, not concurrent mission performance. FIELD02 previously encoded its synthetic source in the Android document, competing for its CPU. The next useful slice is the same cockpit and unchanged installed development APK with an external laptop producer.

A small curated app/UI fork under cockpitvideo04 is preferred to a reverse proxy: the APK only allows localhost8872/8873 and its mirror/lifecycle/SAF contracts must keep their existing origin semantics. Imports retain frozen mission, journal, authority, flight and media algorithms. New topology, measurements and route changes receive separate manifests. Reusing an immutable helper does not promote an earlier result into integrated acceptance.

No physical-camera or Hera performance claim follows. Device and publisher authentication remain engineering localhost assumptions, not a production trust boundary. Video source identity and flight identity are distinct; a sidecar records actual overlapping observations rather than claiming the synthetic source belongs to a real payload exposure. Flight loss revokes authority independently of pixels. No new binary or signing is necessary.
