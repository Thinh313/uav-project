## Architecture and changed surfaces

`PayloadCore97.java` holds routing, capabilities, ownership, bounded explicit intents and independent observations. `UnifiedCore98.java` and `PayloadNative97.java` join those controls to the existing sole `DirectTransport95` socket, sequence, generation and raw WAL. `NativeSend98.java` preserves durable TX_INTENT before current policy and one socket write, followed by TX_WRITTEN. `DirectTransport95.java` retains all raw RX bytes; derived poll-clock-only fields do not force unchanged semantic snapshot records.

`operator01.mjs`, `operator-model01.mjs`, the stylesheet and same-origin policy implement the main cockpit workflow. `presentation-owner01.mjs` prevents legacy mock/flight painters from overwriting native controls and labels; one Fly paint flow updates the memoized native projection. Old fake shutter/zoom/shot-count behavior is removed from those owned controls. Capture, zoom and focus buttons reflect actual advertised capability and baseline requirements. Main Fly shows actual media count, while the larger panel reports intent counts and uncertainty.

`peers.mjs` is a synthetic bounded camera/gimbal fixture, not physical equipment. Its URL decoding repair uses `fileURLToPath`; prior pre-arm failure is preserved. Root owns runtime ports, processes, admission, launch and shutdown.

## Known limits

Camera commands use bounded one-attempt best-effort semantics; they do not implement automatic reliable-command retransmission. ACK ambiguity remains explicit. Gimbal control is explicit absolute setpoints, not continuous joystick/rate control or deadman-certified streaming. Two local actors demonstrate role and consent logic; they are not authenticated remote stations. Payload observations are synthetic. No real image, HeraHub, Phase One SDK, gimbal hardware, rain/wind/loaded-Hera accuracy, production signature, physical flight safety, 24-hour endurance acceptance or DJI superiority has been established by this milestone. Hardware integration and those product outcomes remain subsequent milestones.
