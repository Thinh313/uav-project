P3 UAV Camera delivering  the finest details from the furthest distance
Designed to maximize the productivity of any inspection project where time, safety, and image details are paramount for success, P3 UAV Camera provides you with the right tools to achieve unmatched performance.
Featuring a medium format sensor 1.7 times larger than other cameras, Phase One iXM captures more details in each frame saving you tremendous mission time.
Our iXM cameras are uniquely designed for UAVs and are fast, highly responsive, robust, and easily integrate with your
aerial photography drone
for diverse inspection and mapping missions.
iXM cameras
are available with a range of RSM lenses to ensure that your Phase One P3 UAV Camera gives you the best image quality and performance.
