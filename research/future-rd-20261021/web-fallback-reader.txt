L51: # Hadron 640
L52: The NDAA compliant and ITAR free Hadron™ 640 series pairs a 64 MP visible camera with a performance-leading 640x512 resolution radiometric Boson® or Boson+ thermal camera in a single easy-to-integrate module. With a size, weight, and power (SWaP) optimized design, it is an ideal dual sensor payload for integration into unmanned aircraft systems (UAS), unmanned ground vehicles (UGV), robotic platforms, and emerging AI applications using Teledyne FLIR Prism™ software.
L53: Hadron 640 models share mechanical and electrical interfaces simplifying design. Compatible with Teledyne FLIR’s Prism AI detection, tracking, and classification models and Prism ISP libraries for super-resolution, turbulence mitigation, contrast enhancement, and more, Hadron 640 series enables effective AI-based applications.
L54: With drivers available for market leading processors from NVIDIA®, Qualcomm®, and more plus industry-leading integration support, Hadron 640 series minimizes development cost and time to market.
L55: Evaluate Prism with the Hadron 640 series using the Prism Development Kit for Qualcomm RB5 today.
